Chicken thighs with wilted spinach and yogurt sauce

35 min · 2 servings · macros estimated per serving

{{m.value}}
{{m.label}}

From your fridge

Chicken thighs · Today Baby spinach · Tomorrow Greek yogurt · 2–3 days

Steps

  1. Season the thighs with salt and pepper; sear skin-down 8 min until crisp.
  2. Flip and cook 6 min more.
  3. Wilt the spinach in the pan juices, 2 min.
  4. Season the yogurt with salt; spoon over to serve.

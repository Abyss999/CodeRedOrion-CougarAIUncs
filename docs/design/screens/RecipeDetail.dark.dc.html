From your fridge

Chicken thighs · today Baby spinach · 1 day Greek yogurt · 3 days

You'll need: 1 lemon, 3 garlic cloves

Steps

  1. Season the thighs with salt and pepper; sear skin-down 8 min until crisp.
  2. Flip, add sliced garlic, cook 6 min more.
  3. Wilt the spinach in the pan juices, 2 min.
  4. Stir lemon juice into the yogurt; spoon over to serve.
